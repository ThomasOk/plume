# 💬 Comments and reactions

A memo can start a conversation. Readers comment underneath, and anyone signed in reacts with one emoji from a small, friendly set: 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 💡 🙏 😢

Hover a reaction — or long-press it on a phone — to see who left it. A memo's author is notified of each comment — in the app, and by email unless they turned it off.
